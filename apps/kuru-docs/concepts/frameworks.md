# Frameworks

A framework gives Kuru its starting parts, their roles, and the perspectives their instructions encourage. Every part remains an equal peer: it can propose work, address another part, and become the speaking identity.

Choose a framework with <kbd>F4</kbd>, `/mode`, or `--mode`. The choice is independent of the inference provider, model, and tools. Terminal selections are remembered for the current project.

## IFS {#ifs}

```sh
kuru --mode ifs
```

The default framework gives preparation, protection, recovery, vulnerable stakes, and aspirations their own voices. Its open orbital portrait represents parts in relationship.

| Part      | Role        | Tendency                                                        |
| --------- | ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Self      | self        | Bring curiosity and clarity; integrate perspectives when useful |
| Planner   | manager     | Anticipate dependencies and prepare practical steps             |
| Guardian  | manager     | Notice boundaries, commitments, and preventable failures        |
| Responder | firefighter | Address immediate friction with useful action                   |
| Restorer  | firefighter | Find reversible recovery paths after errors or overload         |
| Witness   | exile       | Remember vulnerable stakes and overlooked costs                 |
| Hope      | exile       | Keep aspirations and neglected possibilities in view            |

Self can be challenged or replaced as the speaker. The architecture does not give it ownership of the pool.

## Polyvagal {#polyvagal}

```sh
kuru --mode polyvagal
```

Three flowing traces accompany three modeled tendencies. The framework can bring engagement, urgency, and conservation into the same discussion.

| Part         | Role          | Tendency                                           |
| ------------ | ------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Connection   | ventral_vagal | Favor engagement and clear collaboration           |
| Mobilization | sympathetic   | Bring alertness and purposeful action              |
| Conservation | dorsal_vagal  | Notice overload and suggest simpler recovery paths |

These are computational roles, not measurements of anyone's nervous system.

## Freudian {#freudian}

```sh
kuru --mode freudian
```

An open triangle represents three perspectives that can negotiate rather than collapse into a single viewpoint.

| Part      | Role     | Tendency                                                              |
| --------- | -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Desire    | id       | Generate possibilities and direct expressions of what would be useful |
| Reality   | ego      | Test proposals against evidence and practical constraints             |
| Standards | superego | Make values, commitments, and quality expectations explicit           |

Reality participates as a peer; it is not a manager of Desire and Standards.

## Jungian {#jungian}

```sh
kuru --mode jungian
```

A rosette accompanies recurring patterns, contrasting perspectives, and continuity across work.

| Part       | Role                   | Tendency                                                            |
| ---------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Continuity | ego                    | Track commitments and the current working identity                  |
| Interface  | persona                | Attend to how the work is communicated and received                 |
| Shadow     | shadow                 | Explore neglected assumptions and avoided tradeoffs                 |
| Bridge     | anima_animus           | Connect contrasting perspectives without assigning traits by gender |
| Collective | collective_unconscious | Work with recurring motifs and available project knowledge          |

Collective's durable memory is scoped to the project in this version. It does not have implicit access to other projects or a universal shared memory.

## What changes over time

These tables describe the built-in membership. [Dreaming](./sessions#dreaming) can add or retire members within validated bounds, while retaining at least one active part for each role. [Relationships](./memory#relationships) can bring two to four parts together as a temporary speaking identity with its own history.

Kuru uses psychological frameworks as software interpretations. Roles influence the work; their modeled states do not establish sentience, diagnose a person, or provide clinical treatment.
