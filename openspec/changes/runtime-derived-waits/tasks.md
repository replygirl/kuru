Every site below is recorded as done (with its shape and source) or not changed (with
a code-backed reason) when its task is ticked. A shape written here is the intended
one; the implementer confirms it against the code and records what was done. Rules
for every task: each wait is event-driven and bounded by a budget derived from a
stated product value, with the derivation at the site or in the constant's doc
comment; no literal without a derivation; no retry; no change to
`startup_timeout_secs`; no product code; no edit to `progress_wait.rs`,
`dream_gap_bound`, `quiesce_bound` or `turn_admission_deadline`. "Filtered run"
means `mise run //packages/kuru-runtime:test -- <test name>`, whose arguments reach
`cargo test`. Edits stay local to the cited lines (PR #208 overlaps these files).
