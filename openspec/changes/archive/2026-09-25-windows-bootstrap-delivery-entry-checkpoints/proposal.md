## Why

Two exact-head Windows delivery fixtures timed out during their first stock PowerShell invocation without enough phase evidence to tell whether the direct installer or encoded wrapper had begun. Their existing bounded authority and refusal assertions must remain intact while the failure phase is located.

## What Changes

- Enable the installer's existing verbose direct checkpoints on the first `-File` invocation in the alias/refusal fixture.
- Add flushed, fixed-text entry and boundary checkpoints around the first encoded wrapper's dot-sourced recovery and native fault call.

## Impact

Touches only `packages/kuru-delivery/tests/bootstrap_windows.rs`; both invocation forms, time bounds, and success/refusal assertions remain unchanged. Exact native Windows CI is still required and its prior timeout cause is not yet established.
